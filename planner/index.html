How This Optimizer Works

1. Calculator = Master Source
Your Calculator determines which characters, bosses and difficulties you can clear. The Optimizer reads that data instead of creating its own selections.

2. Difficulty Sync
If your Calculator has Lotus N selected, the Optimizer gets Lotus N. If you have Lotus H selected instead, it gets Lotus H. If you have both selected, both are synced.

3. Only Selected Crystals Are Eligible
If you cannot clear Carling, Limbo, Baldrix, Jupiter, etc., and they are not selected in the Calculator, they will not become optimization candidates even if their crystal values are higher.

4. Maximum 12 Weekly Crystals
For each character, the Optimizer ranks the crystals you actually selected and chooses the best 12 based on their meso value after party splitting.

5. Party Size
Party size is synced using the exact Character + Boss + Difficulty combination from the Calculator.

6. Different Characters Can Have Different Party Sizes
For example, Character A can have Lotus N ÷1 while Character B can have Lotus N ÷3. The Optimizer keeps those values separate.

7. Optimization Does Not Modify Your Calculator
The Optimizer only calculates which of your available crystals provide the highest income. It does not change your Calculator selections.

8. If You Change Your Calculator
Return here and press ↻ Sync from Calculator. The Optimizer will rebuild its candidate list from the updated Calculator data.
