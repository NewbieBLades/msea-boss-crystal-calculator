How synchronization works:
The MSEA Boss Crystal Calculator is the master source for boss information and clear status. If you select or deselect a boss in the Calculator, this Planner will reflect that selection. Changing the party size in the Calculator will also update here.

Automatic reset system:
Weekly: All Weekly Boss selections are automatically cleared every Thursday at 00:00 GMT+8.
Monthly: All Monthly Boss selections are automatically cleared on the 1st of every month at 00:00 GMT+8.

Resetting selections does not delete:
• Accounts
• Characters
• Character names
• Party sizes
• Personal boss notes
• Boss data

The reset only removes the corresponding Weekly or Monthly boss clear selections.

Weekly selection limits:
Each character can have a maximum of 12 weekly Boss Crystals. There is no account-wide selection limit in the Planner.

Important:
The Planner does not apply the 90-crystal selling limit. The separate Optimizer can use the 90-crystal limit when calculating which selected weekly Boss Crystals should be sold.

How Notes work:
Each boss has its own personal Note box.

• Party IGN
• Carry IGN
• Duo partner
• Party size reminder
• "Ask guild"
• "Need to clear before reset"
• Any other personal reminder

Notes are saved automatically in your browser and are separate for each account, character and boss.
