How synchronization works:
The MSEA Boss Crystal Calculator is the master source for boss information and clear status. If you select or deselect a boss in the Calculator, this Planner will reflect that selection. Changing the party size in the Calculator will also update here.

Weekly selection limits:
Each character can have a maximum of 12 weekly Boss Crystals. There is no account-wide selection limit in the Planner. You can therefore have, for example:
• Character 1 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 2 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 3 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 4 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 5 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 6 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 7 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 8 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 9 — 12 weekly clears
• Character 10 — 12 weekly clears

All selected clears remain visible and synchronized.

Important:
The Planner does not apply the 90-crystal selling limit. The separate Optimizer can use the 90-crystal limit when calculating which selected weekly Boss Crystals should be sold.

How Notes work:
Each boss has its own personal Note box. You can type anything you want, such as:
• Party IGN
• Carry IGN
• Duo partner
• Party size reminder
• "Ask guild"
• "Need to clear before reset"
• Any other personal reminder
Notes are saved automatically in your browser and are separate for each account, character and boss.
