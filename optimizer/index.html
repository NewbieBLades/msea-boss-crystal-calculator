How the Optimizer Works
1. Character and Boss Crystal data are read from the Calculator.

2. Every weekly boss difficulty is treated as a possible clear.

3. Each difficulty can be cleared up to 2 times.

4. Your actual income is calculated as:
Boss Crystal Value ÷ Party Size

5. The optimizer sorts all possible clears from highest meso-per-person to lowest.

6. It then takes the best 12 clears for each character.

7. Monthly Black Mage income is not counted toward the 12 weekly crystal slots.

8. The Calculator remains the master source. The Optimizer does not modify Calculator data.
