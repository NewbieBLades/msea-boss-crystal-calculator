How This Optimizer Works

1. Calculator = Master Source
Your Calculator determines which characters, bosses and difficulties you can clear. The Optimizer reads that data instead of creating its own boss list.

2. Only Selected Crystals Are Eligible
If you cannot clear Carling, Limbo, Baldrix, Jupiter, etc., and they are not selected in the Calculator, they will not appear as optimization candidates even if their crystal values are higher.

3. Best 90 Weekly Crystals Per Account
Across each account, the Optimizer ranks all weekly crystals actually selected in the Calculator and chooses the best 90 based on their meso value after party splitting.

4. Party Size
Crystal value is divided by the party size configured in the Calculator.

5. Optimization Does Not Modify Your Calculator
The Optimizer only calculates which of your available crystals provide the highest income. It does not change your Calculator selections.

6. If You Change Your Calculator
Return here and press ↻ Sync from Calculator. The Optimizer will rebuild its candidate list from the updated Calculator data.
