How the Optimizer Works
1. Every weekly boss difficulty is treated as a possible clear.

2. Each difficulty can be cleared up to 2 times.

3. Your actual income is calculated as:
Boss Crystal Value ÷ Party Size

4. The optimizer sorts all possible clears from highest meso-per-person to lowest.

5. It then takes the best 12 clears for each character.

6. Monthly Black Mage income is not counted toward the 12 weekly crystal slots.
